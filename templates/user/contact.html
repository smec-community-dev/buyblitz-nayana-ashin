Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take? +
Standard shipping typically takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping (2-3 business days) and overnight shipping are also available for an additional fee. Delivery times may vary during holidays or peak seasons.
What is your return policy? +
We offer a 30-day return policy for most items in original condition. Some items like electronics have a 14-day return window. Items must be unused and in original packaging with all tags attached.
Do you ship internationally? +
Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping times vary by destination and typically take 10-21 business days. Additional customs fees or import taxes may apply depending on your country's regulations.
How can I track my order? +
Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also track your order by logging into your account and visiting the "Order History" section. For additional assistance, contact our customer service team.
